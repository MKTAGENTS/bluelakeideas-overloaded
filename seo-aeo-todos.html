Why these three: Book schema tells Google there's a book here (eligible for Book rich results). Person schema establishes Matt's credentials (E-E-A-T signal, helps AI engines cite him as an authority). FAQ schema lets Google show your answers directly in search and gives AI engines pre-structured Q&A to draw from.
Test your schema at search.google.com/test/rich-results after pasting. Fix any errors before going live.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Quarterly maintenance, ongoing
AEO differs from SEO: Google SEO rewards backlinks + on-page signals. AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google's AI Overview) reward consistency across sources, structured data, and citations from authoritative content. Below is what moves the needle for AI surfacing.

Quarterly AI engine test queries

Run these in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overview. Note which engines surface Matt or the book. Use the results to identify gaps.
